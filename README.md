# Filling In the Blind Spot — triangle rain edition

An interactive perception demo: a field of small flickering triangles with a clean
round hole in it. Position the hole on your eye's blind spot, hold your gaze on the
fixation dot, and your visual system draws the triangles straight across the hole —
completing a pattern it cannot actually see.

A copycat of the classic Sensation & Perception textbook CD demo ("Filling in the
Blind Spot"), re-drawn in Coattails colours (Sunlight golds, Lotus pinks, teal depths).

## Use

Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages site) fullscreen.

1. **Find my blind spot** — cover one eye, stare at the fixation dot, slide
   *Hole distance* until the white locator disc vanishes completely.
2. **Start the rain** — keep staring at the dot. Within 5–20 seconds the empty
   circle floods with triangles and disappears into the field.

Sliders adjust hole distance, hole size, and rain brightness. Right eye is the
default; the fixation point mirrors automatically for the left-eye test.

## Why it works

Where the optic nerve leaves the eye there are no light receptors, so that patch
of the world sends no signal at all. Faced with a simple, consistent pattern around
the gap, the brain fills it in — drawing the surrounding texture across the hole
rather than admitting a blank.
